> a **pistol squat** is a type of **single-leg squat**

i don't remember when this started, 
but i remember my first pistol squat in **may 2024**,

since then, i have been trying to include this into my routine to improve
- mobility, flexibility and balance
- muscle strength on my legs to support my knees
	- *mainly this actually, especially after injuring my left knee while running a few years ago, i read that focusing on leg strength can help alleviate the effort put on the knees*

alongside, 
i have been using the **bosu ball** at the gym to work on my balance, 

little by little,
i managed to pair pistol squats with bosu ball, 

my left knee and leg in general do feel weaker than my right side, 

but currently, 
i can do **3 pistol squats** on the bosu ball for each side, 
i haven't been the most consistent with it,

my goal for 2025 was 5 reps, 
before slowly getting into another type of single-leg squat... 
maybe the *dragon squat* next? 🐉
