### i. why i started
- i was already using notion for most of my notes and journaling 
	- basically my second brain
- but i wanted to try something else, 
	- with a different ecosystem, a different flow
	- i wanted something simpler, more straightforward (i used to use [bear](https://bear.app/) and [evernote](https://evernote.com/) in the past, but dropped them a long time ago)
	- but at the same time, something that allows notes to connect easily, a bit like a network, mind map kind of vibe
- the concepts of digital garden and zettelkasten have always sat in the back of my mind
	-> and obsidian was one of the possible note taking tools for this (cause it has this cool graph tool to connect nodes)
- coincidentally, 
	- i also stumbled upon this video entitled [[creating a digital garden|creating a digital garden to end my doomscrolling]] by Anna Howard
> i thought it was the final nudge for me to try this out

### ii. how i started
- i watched videos to set up my obsidian vault following some sort of organisational system (cf. [[obsidian the king of learning tools]])
	- how to set up the folders
	- atomic notes 
	- source notes 
	- tags system
- this was also a motivation to read more, take more notes, consume... and write more basically 
	- in parallel, i started reading a lot of substack articles (great alternative to social media, would recommend, without falling into the trap of doomscrolling over there too) 
	- *... so yeah one thing leading to another...* 
- i have accumulated a handful of notes in my personal vault, but they sometimes are a little bit too "copy pasted" 
	- cause that's how i read and learn, sometimes i'd just copy paste the content and go through it and highlight things, take notes in the same file...
		- tldr; it's still pretty messy at times.
	- but it is a good base to get started, and i can use it for this place too!
		- *yeah i still need to transfer a bunch of notes from there to here now...* 

### iii. how i like it 
- i have been liking it so far, 
- i like the easy and simple system, no fancy or pretentious feature or layout, nothing complicated or requiring a steep learning curve 
- everything is intuitive, straightforward and focused on the essential purpose: **writing** 
- like i said, i feel like using it opened doors to other stuff as well (substack, digital garden, knowledge)

### iv. whats my goal
- hmm am not sure here, i think it
	- started with curiosity and exploration
	- then, it was about reading more and populating the folders (and at the same time, my mind too)
- the novelty did fizzle out, and i am no longer using it as consistently (daily)
	- but it feels like i have something started if i ever want to pick this back up 
	- and it's always a good thing because sometimes the most daunting thing is to get started with something totally new and unknown-- the friction is higher vs. knowing you already did the heavy lifting of figuring how this works and whatnot... *momentum*

... i guess yeah, i don't have a particular goal with this, i hope i keep using it, 
or maybe i'll start using this 🪴 le jardin 🪴 vault more?
