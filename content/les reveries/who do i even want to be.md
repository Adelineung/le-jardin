following the [[steal-like-an-artist#-start-copying|steal like an artist]] book, 

the question is...
>*who do i even want to be?* 

#### i. make a list of people who inspire you, and why
#### ii. look for for patterns
my thread is: *the beautifully human creator*

3 main pillars: 
1. **deep authenticity, profound humanity** 
	- human, authentic, real, unapologetically genuine, unique, relatable
2. **soothing, positive presence** 
	- calm, peaceful, soothing, comforting, gentle, soft, hopeful, kind, encouraging
3. **masterful craft, inspiring intelligence** 
	- talented, creative, artistic, intelligent, wise, well-spoken, strong 

the people i admire the most sit at the intersection of those pillars,

i am drawn to people who have achieved a high level of skill or self-awareness, 
while retaining their warmth, humility and humanity. 

they are able to make people ***feel*** something through their craft, or simply through who they are:
> peace, inspiration, connection. 

#### iii. how can i learn from this all 
1. self-reflection, look within-- what does this pattern even say about you? and where are you at? 
2. action, embody the traits your admire-- find ways to cultivate what you look up to 
3. educate yourself, never stop being an **==apprentice of life==**
