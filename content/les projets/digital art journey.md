### a journey 
started end of april 2025, 
##### i. motivation 
i like art, 
i have always liked drawing, but did less and less of it as i got older, 

art is beautiful, 
it's freeing and fun to be able to express yourself, to create something, 

i think it's a valuable skill, 
a lovely hobby, 

it's a slow process, 
a very manual one, 

but at the same time,
you do use your **brain**-- to analyse, to observe,
to translate what you see with your eyes or what you imagine in your head to the paper 
with your **hands**,

so, 
it's more than just putting your brain on the side, 
and letting your hands do all the work, 

though i can imagine this becomes more natural, 
the more you practice, 
the more you become accustomed to the technique, 

it's still a journey, 
and it makes me escape for a little bit-- how sweet? 
##### ii. progress, notes

1. i started off with more **interactive tutorials** (videos)
	- -> easier to [[steal-like-an-artist#-start-copying|copy]], especially at the beginning (*[[get-better-at-anything#2-creativity-begins-with-copying|"creativity begins with copying"]]*)
	- better guidance
	- getting to know basics 
2. got more confident and transitioned to **drawing with a reference** (images)
	- more [[get-better-at-anything#5-the-difficulty-sweet-sport|challenging]] because 1 level of freedom to figure out by yourself 
		- the step by step 
		- the tools 
		- the technique 
3. *next: drawing from scratch, **personal creations from A-Z** (void, or ∞)*
	- probably can still use some inspiration and reference, 
	- but it's no longer trying to "replicate"
	- you are free to choose everything, 
		- from the composition 
		- the style 
		- the colours
	- ... 
##### iii. goals
> exploration is enough, just touch on multiple things, learn and have fun
> no pressure :D
