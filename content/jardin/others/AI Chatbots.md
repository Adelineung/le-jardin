- **LLMs**: Large Language Models -- broad AI models trained on massive datasets to **understand and generate human language** (basically NLP models on steroids)
	- GPT-4, Llama, Claude, Mistral, etc. 
	- -> they are general-purpose engines that can perform many language-related tasks beyond chatting, such as translation, code gen, content creation etc. 
- **Chatbot**: specialised AI apps built on or powered by LLMs (or other models), designed for conversation and dialogue => **conversational agents**
	- -> they remember previous interactions, keep context, deliver more coherent and context-aware responses. 
	- -> the traditional chatbots (that are not LLM-powered) often rely on rule-based or template-driven responses and lack flexibility and contextual understanding
