### looong and **thin**
- Capellini (it’s like thin noodle)
- Spaghetti
- Linguine (flat but thin)
- Bucatini (the hollow one)

🍝 SAUCE
- light, smooth but savoury sauces
- tomato-based: marina
- olive oil-based: aglio e olio
- lemon pasta
- cacio e pepe
- carbonara

### looong and BROAD
- Tagliatelle
- Fettuccine
- Pappardelle
- Malfadine (asian noodles vibe)

🍝 SAUCE
- hearty, robust, rich, creamy sauce
- slow-cooked bolognese
- mushroom, meat, ragu
- alfredo
- creamy pesto

### tube pasta
- Macaroni
- Penne
- Ziti (penne, but smooth ridges // long macaroni)
- Rigatoni (cylinder, straight/smooth ridges) 
- Tortiglioni (narrower than rigatoni, spiral ridges)
- Cannelloni (baking) 
- Manicotti (baking)

🍝 SAUCE
- everything apparently
- but great with meaty sauces 

### twiiist shape
- Fusilli
- Rotini (v similar to fusili)
- Cavatappi (curly hair)
- Gemelli (2 braids)

🍝 SAUCE
- chunky sauce
- pesto,
- tuna based
- mix or veggies

### shell shaped
- Conchiglie
- Conchiglioni
- Rocchetti (looks like a hollow gnocchi)

### stuffed
- Ravioli (square)
- Tortellini (ring like)
- Tortelloni (fat)

### others
- Orecchiette (flat shell)
- Lasagna
- Farfalle
- Cavatelli
- Orzo
- Gnocchi
- Ditalini (mini tube)
