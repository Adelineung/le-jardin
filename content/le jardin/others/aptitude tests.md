#### Abstract reasoning

#### Diagrammatic reasoning
-> manipulate shapes with operations (transpose, rotate, size, inverse colour)
#### Inductive reasoning
induction: specific events => generalisation

common patterns
- rotation or flip
- reflection across a line or axis
- alternating 
- progression (increase or decrease)
- repeating
- position (clockwise or anti-clockwise) 
- combination 

#### Deductive reasoning
deduction: general facts -> specific events
