### random notes from youtube videos
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zyi_OxSIoT4
	- culinary vs. ceremonial matcha grade
	    - culinary → smoothie, baking, …
	    - ceremonial → traditional tee form
	- taste and flavours
	    - too bitter or even fishy → could be culinary
	- best matcha:
	    - japan — kyoto or uji (but beware of the marketing, it’s not absolute, some from other places in japan can be good too)
	    - avoid china

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5jIiUw0JXk
	- look out for matcha vs. green tea kinda flavour
	- bad: kinda gritty sensation when tasting, while it should be smooth
	- leaf colour is great!!! like a monstera leaf
	- rich flavour, smooth taste and feeling is great
	- right amount of bitterness is nice
	- grassiness is nice
	- shouldn’t taste like bold dirt
	- “daily” on the packaging usually means a lower grade

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0cMV94EwR2U
	- flavour:
	    - grassy, earthy, deep green tea, can be subtly (or deeply) umami i.e. savoury-ish
	    - high quality → delicious sweetness (can be honey like)
	- scent:
	    - ceremonial → for drinking:
	        - very aromatic
	        - green tea heavy
	        - base earthiness
	    - culinary → darker / less vibrant
