entropy is a measure of **disorder** or **randomness**

- high entropy = high disorder, randomness or spread-out energy
- low entropy = high order, structure, or concentrated energy 

a fundamental law of nature
> **total entropy in the universe always increases over time** 
> in fact, things tend to move from order to disorder on their own
