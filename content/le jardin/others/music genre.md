#### Hip-hop / Rap
- Rhythmic spoken or chanted lyrics (rap) over a backing beat 
- Focus on wordplay, flow, rhythm 

#### Pop
- Catchy melodies, verse-chorus structure
- Designed for mass appeal

#### Rock
- Electric guitars, bass, drums, vocals
- Emphasises energy, riffs, strong backbeat 
- -> driving rhythm of electric guitars + steady drum beat

#### R&B / Soul
- Smooth, emotive vocals
- Focus on melody and groove 
- Roots in gospel and blues
- -> soulful singing, laid-back but rhythmic groove 
##### Neo-soul
- Organic instrumentation (live bass, rhodes piano),
- Jazzy chords, poetic lyrics
- Classic soul x hip-hip and jazz influences 
##### Soul
- Raw, gospel-influenced vocals, prominent backbeat, emotional delivery
- Similar to gospel
##### Funk
- Strong rhythmic groove, prominent bass guitar, syncopated rhythms, horn sections
- -> the groooooove makes you dance
#### Jazz
- Complex harmony, syncopated rhythms, improvisation
- Swing feel and sophisticated chords

#### Blues
- 12-bar chord progression and blues scale
- Lyrical themes of struggle and emotion 

#### Classical
- Orchestral instruments (strings, woodwinds, brass)
- Complex compositions
#### Reggae
- Off-beat rhythmic accent
- Prominent bass lines, relaxed tempo 

#### Country
- Storytelling lyrics, twangy vocals 
- Acoustic guitar, banjo, fiddle

#### Folk
- Acoustic instrumentation, narrative storytelling

#### Latin Music
- Danceable rhythms, percussion-driven 
- Spanish/portuguese lyrics
