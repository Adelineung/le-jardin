### i. what's vo2 max

#### a. definition
> VO2 max is the maximum amount, or volume (V), of oxygen (O2) your body can use as a fuel source for exercise

it's your level of cardio fitness, 
a bit like the horsepower in a car, 

concretely this kinda translates into 
- aerobic capacity 
- cardiorespiratory fitness 
- endurance

#### b. 3 principal components
- lung capacity and heart volume 
- capillary delivery: oxygenated blood transported to muscles by circulatory system 
- muscle efficiency: efficiency of muscle O2 extraction and use from blood 

#### c. how does it work?
- you inhale air 
- lungs take in the O2
- O2 gets into the blood cells (which carry O2, nutrients, hormones)
- heart pumps blood constantly
- blood transport that O2 into muscles (all body cells actually)
- muscles extracts and uses O2 as fuel (to move)

#### d. some units
> Max Heart Rate = 220 - age
> Vo2 max = Max Heart Rate * 0.9

### ii. how to improve
- *Endurance training*: long but low-moderate intensity exercise can increase aerobic capacity
- *High-intensity or interval training*: you need to spend time close to your VO2 max (MaxHR 90%) to develop this value -> your body will adapt and increase it 
	- but recovery periods are crucial 
		- never 2 intense sessions in a row 
		- you need to alternate with low-moderate intensity activities 
	- also in-between intervals: rest 3min, or till zone 2 60-70% MaxHR is good

the sweet spot apparently:
 - 3-5 min intervals performed at/near VO2 max 
 - vs. 
	 - longer but lower intensity than that
	 - shorter but higher intensity than that 

but a good rule of thumb, 
- do a bit of everything 
- balance and diversity are actually key 
- recovery and rest are just as important too 

> If you want to maximize your aerobic capacity, you need to combine 
> - large amounts of low-intensity training with
> - small amounts of high-intensity intervals done at or near VO2max
